        How it works

        Every entry gets an equal slice of the wheel, and the winner is picked with a uniform random draw — the spin animation is just theatre. The result is random, not rigged, and it is for fun decisions only: use it to pick dinner, not to settle anything that matters.

        Everything runs in your browser. Your entries, saved lists and spin history are stored in this browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data clears them too.

        Keyboard: press Enter to add an entry, and Space on the spin button to spin.
